# Privacy Policy for Simple Planner, Tracker: Quiks

Last Updated: July 9, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Simple Planner, Tracker: Quiks ("we," "our," or "the app") handles information when you use our task management application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand how your data is handled.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
All user data, including:
- Task entries and descriptions
- Task completion status
- Task categories and labels
- User preferences and settings
- Archived task data

is stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of this information on our servers. Your task data remains entirely private and under your control on your device.

### Automatically & Transactionally Collected Information
We collect minimal system-required information and transactional metadata to run the app:
- Basic usage statistics (fully anonymized)
- App performance data and crash logs
- Device information (OS version, device model)
- Anonymous purchase details (subscription status, transaction IDs, product IDs)

None of this transactional or performance information includes your task data or personal content.

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- All task data is stored exclusively on your local device.
- No cloud storage or server-side storage is implemented for your personal tasks.
- Task archives are maintained only on your device.

### Data Security
Since all your task data is stored locally:
- You have complete control over your data.
- Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features.
- No risk of server-side data breaches.
- No third-party access to your task information.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your task data never leaves your device. Anonymized system-required information and purchase history are only shared under the following circumstances:
- To facilitate transaction processing and subscription entitlement verification.
- When required by law.
- With your explicit consent.

## Third-Party Services

To provide in-app upgrades and analyze app performance, our app integrates with the following privacy-respecting third-party services:
- **RevenueCat**: Used to securely process and verify in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat handles anonymous transactional details (such as subscription state, transaction dates, and active entitlements). No task data is shared with RevenueCat.
- **TelemetryDeck**: Used for privacy-first, anonymous usage analytics to help us improve the app. TelemetryDeck does not track personally identifiable information (PII) or task content. All analytics data is aggregate and fully anonymized.
- **Apple (System Tools)**: Used for native crash reporting and processing payments through the App Store.

Each third-party service is governed by its own privacy policy.

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:
- Access your task data (stored locally on your device).
- Delete your data from the app by purging tasks or uninstalling the app.
- Opt out of anonymous analytics collection.
- Restore previously purchased upgrades via the paywall screen.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or maintain information from persons under 13 years of age. If you are under 13, please do not use the app.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app or linking to the latest version.
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

## California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). However, please note that we do not collect or store your personal information on our servers.
